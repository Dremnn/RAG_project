# AI Engineering Cheat Sheet

1. Data Preparation:
- Split raw documents with recursive or semantic chunking.
- Maintain overlap (e.g., 50-100 characters/tokens) to preserve boundary context.

2. Retrieval Optimization:
- Combine Dense Vector search (Jina, BAAI, OpenAI) with Sparse Lexical search (BM25).
- Normalise BM25 scores before reciprocal rank fusion or weighted sum.

3. Evaluation:
- Decompose generated responses into atomic claims.
- Evaluate Faithfulness and Groundedness using an LLM-as-a-judge.
